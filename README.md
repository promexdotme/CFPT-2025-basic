## Is the free edition worth studying with?

Yes — absolutely. This app covers all four exam sections with realistic question formats that mirror what you'll see on test day. Use it to:

- **Familiarize yourself with the exam structure** — same four sections, same timing pressure, same question styles
- **Test your instincts on Comprehending Regulations** — passage-based logic questions drawn from INA and 14 FAM language
- **Practice English Expression** — sentence-level grammar, punctuation, and word-choice questions
- **Review Job Knowledge** — U.S. government, history, international relations, and economics
- **Get a feel for Situational Judgment** — workplace scenarios testing professional judgment and ethics

If you're early in your prep or just want to gauge where you stand, start here.

### Why the Best/Worst format matters

The real CFPT Situational Judgment section requires you to select **both** the most effective **and** most harmful response from four options — you only earn the point if both are correct. That format is one of the most disorienting parts of the exam for unprepared candidates. The 2026 book trains you on it specifically across all 100 SJ questions.

## About the CFPT

The Consular Fellows Program Test is a competitive exam for the U.S. Department of State's Consular Fellows Program. The exam covers:
- **Section 1:** Comprehending Regulations
- **Section 2:** English Expression
- **Section 3:** Job Knowledge
- **Section 4:** Situational Judgment

Pass threshold: ≥70% on each section. Results provided immediately. Administered on-demand at Pearson VUE testing centers.
